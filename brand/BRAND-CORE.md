# উর্বরা · Urbora: brand core (v1, 2026-09-30)

**This is the single source of truth** for name, story, tagline, voice and look, for every
session (website, video, form, deck). Where older files (BRAND.md, VOICE.md, NAMES.md,
VIDEO_HANDOFF.md) disagree with this page, this page wins.

Replaced names, never to be used again: Agrosfeer, AgriSense (hackathon), Jigai, Sudin/সুদিন
(bad homophone), Urbor/উর্বর (a Dhaka agri-fintech is listed as "Urbor" on Crunchbase), Urborota/উর্বরতা
(four syllables, abstract; "fertility" reads as IVF in English).

---

## 1. Name

| | |
|---|---|
| Bangla (primary, always larger or equal) | **উর্বরা** |
| Latin | **Urbora** (say: ur-bo-ra) |
| Meaning | Fertile. It is the word used for land in songs and poems ("উর্বরা মাটি"). |

**Why this name.**
- Fertile soil is Bangladesh's biggest natural gift, and that gift is exactly what farmers
  are losing. The name describes the land we help keep fertile.
- It is short (3 syllables), ends like a name, and is easy for judges to say.
- "Urbora" echoes *aurora*, the dawn, and so matches the seal's rising sun.
- Collision check (web search, 2026-09-30, not a trademark clearance): "Urbora" is an
  Assamese bookshop in Guwahati (different country and sector). urbora.com is listed for sale.
  The Dhaka agri-fintech "Urbor" is one letter away, so always lead with the Bangla
  wordmark, and in English always write "Urbora" in full, next to the seal.

## 2. Tagline (the brand line, for years)

**স্বপ্ন ফলুক**. English for judges: *"May your dreams bear fruit."*

ফলা means both "a harvest grows" and "a dream comes true". It is a wish, like an elder's
blessing, and it points forward: a better future, not "getting the old days back".
Lockup: **উর্বরা · স্বপ্ন ফলুক**. It is set in Galada (hand-lettered) only.

## 3. The story (the essence)

**Bangla, for voice-over and the website** (a starting point; the team rewrites it in its own words):

> এ দেশের মাটি উর্বর। এই উর্বরতাই আমাদের সবচেয়ে বড় উপহার।
> কিন্তু একই জমিতে আগের মতো ফলন আর আসছে না। কোথাও মাটির পুষ্টি কমছে,
> কোথাও রোগ-পোকা বাড়ছে, আর কৃষক বুঝতে পারছেন না ঠিক কী হয়েছে।
> জানার জায়গা আছে, কিন্তু কৃষি অফিস অনেক দূরে।
> মোবাইল ব্যাংকিং যেমন ব্যাংককে হাতের মুঠোয় এনেছে, উর্বরা তেমনি
> কৃষি বিশেষজ্ঞের পরামর্শ কৃষকের ফোনে পৌঁছে দেয়:
> ফসলের সমস্যা চিনে, তাঁর জমির মাপে সঠিক সমাধান।
> উর্বরা। স্বপ্ন ফলুক।

**English (for the form and judges).** Bangladesh's fertile soil is its greatest gift, and
it is slipping. The same land gives less every year. Soil nutrients run down, pests and
diseases rise, and the farmer can't tell which one is hurting his crop. The expertise exists,
but the agriculture office is far away. Mobile banking brought the bank to people's hands;
Urbora brings the agriculture expert to the farmer's phone. It names the problem and
gives the exact fix for his own land.

### The four truths the story stands on (from FIELD_INSIGHTS.md)

1. **The gift is fading.** Hybrids raised yields 15–20 years ago; for ten years the same land
   gives less while costs rise. One farmer: "50 মণ last year, 30 মণ this year" (his figures;
   quote as "a farmer in Dinajpur told us", never as a statistic).
2. **He can't name the problem.** "What disease is this?" He can't describe it well enough
   for the shopkeeper, so he can't choose the right fertiliser or medicine.
3. **Everyone says something different.** Shopkeeper, neighbour and father's habit all give
   different advice. He is confused, not ignorant.
4. **Help is far.** The upazila agriculture office is a day's trip. He is trying; distance
   makes it hard.

### Framing rules (hard)

- Farmers are **trying** to reach help, and distance makes it hard. Never say a hotline
  fails, never mention 16123, never blame officers, shops or government.
- Farmers are capable decision-makers, never victims. No pity, no despair, no rescue story.
- Forward-looking and warm, like a Bangladeshi ad (bKash, GP "চলো বহুদূর"). Spoken Bangla.
- Every number on screen comes from the real system or a named source (CLAUDE.md rules 1–3).

## 5. Look

**Logo.** A round seal: the sun on the horizon, ploughed furrows running toward it, a new
sprout at the centre (the team's pick on 2026-09-30, concept B in the Figma board). Fertile
land, a new day, a new crop. It is being refined (Canva) with the engraved line texture.
Final files go to `brand/assets/logo/`. Until then use
`brand/assets/logo/concepts/mark-b.svg`.

**Colour (palette v2: one scene, one light).**

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| গভীর শ্যামল Deep Shyamol | `#0D3B2E` | Primary, dark grounds, headings, the seal |
| হলুদ রোদ Turmeric Sun | `#F2A231` | The sun in the logo, one CTA, key numbers (≈8%) |
| পাতা Leaf | `#1B7048` | Links, secondary actions |
| কচি ধান Young Paddy | `#A8D65C` | "Recovering / healthy", highlights on dark only |
| খড় Straw | `#FAF5EA` | Page ground |
| Kraft (pattern ground) | `#EBD9B4` | Engraved-pattern panels, print, video backgrounds |
| Sepia line | `#6B4A1E` | Engraved line art on kraft |
| মাটি Soil Ink | `#11201A` | Text |
| পোড়ামাটি Terracotta | `#B8472A` | Real warnings only |

**Type.**

| Role | Font |
|---|---|
| Bangla display, the wordmark | Noto Serif Bengali (Bold/SemiBold) |
| Bangla text, UI, SMS | Noto Sans Bengali |
| Latin display | Fraunces |
| Latin text | Instrument Sans |
| Tagline only | Galada |

**Pattern.** Engraved grain line art on kraft, in the style of the team's references: bold
outlined ears with inner hatching and fine awns. It is drawn as rice paddy, not wheat,
because farmers here would notice. It is used behind heroes, on section breaks and on the video end card.

**Never.** Gradients, glassmorphism, robots or "AI" imagery, Akash blue as a brand colour,
wheat fields, pity imagery.

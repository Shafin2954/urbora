# Name candidates (checked 2026-09-29)

**How they were checked.** Web search for each name plus "Bangladesh" and "app / company /
brand". Where relevant I also checked agriculture specifically. This finds existing apps,
companies and pages; **it is not a legal clearance.** Before final use:

- **RJSC** name clearance (roc.gov.bd)
- **DPDT** trademark search (dpdt.gov.bd)
- `.com.bd` / `.com` domain
- Facebook page name
- Play Store search

## Ten names with no existing organisation found

| # | Bangla | Latin | Meaning and why it fits | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **জিগাই** | Jigai | "Let me ask": what farmers actually say (the Dinajpur farmer: "জিগাইছিলাম"). An open invitation, easy to say, memorable like bKash. Ad line: "কিছু জানার থাকলে, জিগাই-কে জিগান।" | Colloquial and regional; judges may find it informal (so is bKash). |
| 2 | **জানাশোনা** | Janashona | "The one who knows", the trusted informed person in a village. The strongest trust signal; describes our role exactly. | Four syllables; less punchy in Latin. |
| 3 | **হদিস** | Hodish | "The clue / finding the way": "হদিস মিলল" = I found the answer. Elegant and short. | Latin "Hodish" can be misread as "Hadith"; the Bangla is clear. |
| 4 | **বুঝলাম** | Bujhlam | "Now I understand": the relief moment after confusion. Very emotional; the video's last line writes itself. | A verb as a name is unusual ("Bujhlam-এ ফোন দিন" still works). |
| 5 | **চাষি ভাই** | Chashi Bhai | "Farmer brother": warm, familiar, respectful. Sounds like a person, not a company. | Two words; a little generic; male-coded (women farmers exist). |
| 6 | **মাঠকথা** | Mathkotha | "Talk of the field": conversation about the land; fits voice and SMS. | "-kotha" echoes the big Kotha app. |
| 7 | **চাষকথা** | Chashkotha | "Farming talk": the same idea, plainer. | Same Kotha echo. |
| 8 | **ফলবে** | Folbe | "It will grow / it will pay off": hope and promise in one word. Short, positive. | Promise-like; must not read as a yield guarantee. |
| 9 | **মাঠভরা** | Mathbhora | "A field full (of crop)": abundance, the outcome farmers want. | Describes the result, not the service. |
| 10 | **জমিন** | Jomin | "Land" (colloquial): grounded and short. | Too plain on its own; needs a tagline. |

## Recommendation

**Top three, in order:**

1. **জিগাই (Jigai)**
   - It comes from the farmer's own mouth and it *is* the action: ask.
   - It is the kind of word that makes an ad catchy and a brand feel like "ours", as bKash
     did with a spoken sound.
   - Pair it with a calm, trustworthy visual identity (serif, deep green), so the name is
     friendly and the look is serious.
2. **জানাশোনা (Janashona)** if the team wants trust first and playfulness second.
3. **হদিস (Hodish)** if the team wants short and elegant.

**Tagline pairing.**
- **জিগাই · জেনে বুঝে চাষ** ("ask first, farm knowing") reads as one thought.
- Note that "নিশ্চিন্তে চাষ" now overlaps a Gazipur business called Nishchinto Agro, so prefer
  "জেনে বুঝে চাষ".

**Quick gut test** (10 minutes, worth it): read the three names to two or three people from
the village (Shafin Bhai's mama). Ask which one they would remember tomorrow and which one
they would trust. Pick the one that wins both.

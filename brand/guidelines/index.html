যে জানে, সোজা বলে।

The farmer hears many voices: the seller, the neighbour, what his father did. Our brand is the one calm voice that knows his land and tells him straight. Everything below serves that trust.

একবার জিগান, সোজা উত্তর পান, নিজের জমির হিসাবে।Ask once, get a straight answer, for your own land.

Colour

Four colours from the Bengali landscape. Green is home and growth, blue is the phone people already trust, gold is the ripe paddy and is used sparingly, ivory is the silt of the ground.

Why it will be trusted

Village trust comes fromSo the brand
Having nothing to sellNever pushes a product; nobody can pay to rank higher, and we say so.
Being right, specificallyGives his land's numbers in bigha and মণ, with the source.
Admitting limitsSays "তথ্য যথেষ্ট নয়" instead of guessing.
FamiliarityField colours, spoken Bangla, "ভাই", not a foreign tech product.
CalmNo alarm colours except real warnings; unhurried layouts.
RespectFarmers shown as capable, never pitiable.

Do and don't

Do

  • Deep green fields, white space, one clear action
  • Real morning light, hands, paddy, a phone in a weathered hand
  • Spoken Bangla, short sentences, exact numbers
  • Gold only for the one thing that matters on the screen

Don't

  • Gradients, glows, shadows, glass effects
  • Robots, circuit brains, glowing "AI" imagery
  • Pity images, crying farmers, cracked earth
  • Formal Bangla ("প্রদান করা হইবে"), English jargon to farmers

Name and logo

Ten checked names are in brand/NAMES.md. The recommendation is জিগাই (Jigai) with the tagline জেনে বুঝে চাষ. The logo brief, with three concepts and a prompt, is in brand/LOGO-BRIEF.md. The logo stays green and gold; blue belongs to the phone.

## 3. New line-art plates (mg.html, sepia on kraft, drawn on stroke by stroke)

- **P1 "every time" (4 s):** road and office from S06 (static, already drawn). The figure walks to the office and
  back three times at speed; each loop pops a problem icon over the field (a bug, a yellow leaf, a rain cloud) and
  a tally mark; on the third, the tally is crossed through.
- **P2 "why not on the phone too?" (4 s):** left, the ৳ phone (from S07), a tick; right, a phone outline draws in
  with a paddy leaf on its screen; a dotted line runs between them and stops halfway; a big hand-drawn "?" grows
  in the gap and pulses on the question's last word.
- **P3 "how it reaches you" (2.5 s):** button phone (left) → three signal arcs → the seal in a circle (centre) →
  a data stack of books with a tick → an SMS envelope flies back along the arc to the phone.
- **P4 "why trust it?" (2.2 s):** an SMS bubble draws in; a magnifying glass sweeps across it; a "?" stamps.
- **P5 trust, redrawn (7 s):** government building with a data table → arrow → an open book with a tick
  (verified source) → arrow → a speech bubble "জানি না, অফিসে দেখান" (the honest fallback, taken from the SMS text:
  "স্প্রের আগে স্থানীয় কৃষি অফিসার (SAAO)-কে দেখান"). Labels arrive with the words.
- **P6 "in everyone's hands" (3 s):** a drawn field horizon; hands holding phones rise one after another
  (button phones and smartphones), about twelve across the frame, on "এখন সবার হাতে".

The edit is cut to the music: find each track's section boundaries and downbeats first, place scene changes on
them, and cut across tracks only on a reveal (the seal, the whip-pan, the end card). Level: music under the voice
−11 dB (short-term), up to −4 dB in the gaps between lines; no ducking pumping (slow 250 ms release).

## 5. Voice (speech-to-speech from Jawat's recordings)

1. Split the recording into takes by silence; transcribe each take and match it to the script by similarity. Keep
   the take with the best similarity and energy (pitch spread).
2. Denoise lightly (high-pass 80 Hz, spectral gate), level-match.
3. Convert the narrator lines to a deep, warm male preset voice, and the characters to distinct voices (Mahmud
   stays closest to Jawat's own; the shopkeeper, neighbour and video each get a different one). Speech-to-speech
   keeps his timing and accent.
4. Check: the transcription similarity of each converted line must not drop more than 0.05 below its source; a
   listen-test sheet goes to Jawat.

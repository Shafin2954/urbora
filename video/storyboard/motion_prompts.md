# Motion prompts: live-action shots (image-to-video, Kling 3.0 pro, 16:9, sound off)

Written the way a DP and an editor would brief a shot: what the lens does, what moves and what
must not, how the shot has to cut against its neighbours, and what would ruin it. Each clip is
animated from its approved keyframe (`start_image`), so the prompt describes **motion only**; the
look is already in the frame.

## House rules for every clip

- **Camera grammar.** One move per shot, slow and motivated: a dolly or push-in at walking speed
  or slower, or a locked-off tripod. No whip pans, no orbit shots, no speed ramps, no handheld
  shake unless the shot says so. The film is calm; the edit supplies the rhythm.
- **Performance.** Hands move with purpose and economy, the way a working farmer's hands do.
  Nobody turns to camera. No faces appear at any point.
- **Continuity.** Keep the keyframe's light direction, colour temperature and depth of field for
  the whole clip. Nothing new enters frame unless the prompt says so. Objects keep their shape:
  the phone stays a small black button phone, bottles stay unlabelled.
- **Physics.** Rice leaves move in soft, irregular wind, never in unison. Mist drifts, dust floats,
  steam rises and thins. Water ripples only where wind touches it.
- **Cut points.** Every clip needs a clean first second and a clean last second (no morphing at
  the ends), because the edit trims into both.
- **Negative, in every prompt:** no text, no captions, no logos, no faces, no camera shake, no
  morphing, no extra fingers, no sudden lighting change.

### 0 · S01 Dawn over the boro (10 s)
Slow dolly-in along the line of the earthen ridge at walking pace, lens height about 1.2 m,
the horizon held level for the whole shot. The farmer keeps walking away from us at an unhurried
pace, getting slightly smaller; his gamcha shifts with each step. Low mist drifts right to left
across the paddy, the sun lifts a hair above the horizon and the glints on the water between the
rows shimmer. Two or three birds cross high and far. The opening shot of the film: it should feel
like a breath in.

### 1 · S02–S03 The dry shoot (10 s)
Macro, 100mm feel, focus fixed on the dry central shoot. For the first three seconds the hand
parts the tillers a little further and holds, the fingers still. Then a very slow push-in toward
the brown shoot so it grows to fill the centre third of the frame while the hand drifts softly
out of focus. Dew catches the light; the green leaves tremble in faint wind, the dead shoot barely
moves because it is dry and stiff. The last four seconds hold nearly still: the narrator's
question plays over this, so the frame must be calm enough to sit under a line of voice.

### 2 · S04a Shop counter (5 s)
Locked-off tripod, counter-top height. The shopkeeper's hand finishes setting the bottle down, a
small settle, then lets go and withdraws out of frame right. The bottle stays centred and in
focus. Background shelves stay soft and still apart from light dust in the daylight. The motion
completes in the first two seconds so the edit can cut on the release.

### 3 · S04b Tea glass (5 s)
Locked-off. Thin steam curls up from the glass and thins out; the tea's surface trembles very
slightly. In the soft background, blurred shapes shift a little as people talk (no faces). Warm
light stays constant. Nothing else moves.

### 4 · S04c Phone video on the sill (5 s)
Locked-off. The phone screen's glow flickers gently as the blurred video on it changes; its light
plays softly on the window bars. Outside, out of focus, the rice moves in wind. The phone never
moves and its screen content stays blurry and unreadable.

### 5 · S04d Father's sack (5 s)
Very slow push-in, barely perceptible, toward the folded sack. Dust motes turn slowly in the beam
of light. The beam itself stays fixed. Faded, still, like a memory; nothing else moves.

### 6 · S05 Empty field (5 s)
Locked-off wide. Wind moves across the rice in soft travelling waves from left to right, the
palm fronds sway lazily, a thin haze sits over the far edge. No people, no birds in the
foreground. Calm and a little empty: this is where the voices fade to silence.

### 7 · S09 Keypad (5 s)
Locked-off insert, shallow focus on the keys. The thumb presses three keys at an even, practised
rhythm, then rests. The phone stays steady in the hand. The small screen stays dark. The paddy
behind remains a soft green blur with a slight sway.

### 8 · S10 Loudspeaker plate (10 s)
Locked-off, soft backlight. The hand holds the phone steady at chest height the whole time, with
the tiny natural sway of a person listening; once, around the middle, the thumb shifts its grip.
Behind, the paddy moves gently and the edge of the gamcha lifts in the breeze. This plate sits
under the call audio, so it must be quiet and continuous: no gestures, no cuts, no change of
framing.

### 9 · S12a Phone on the counter (5 s)
Top-down, locked-off. The farmer's hand finishes laying the phone flat and withdraws. From the
top of frame the shopkeeper's hand slides the phone a few centimetres toward himself and pauses,
reading. The phone's screen stays evenly lit and blank (the SMS is composited later), and the
phone keeps its exact shape for tracking.

### 10 · S12b One bottle (5 s)
Locked-off, the same angle as S04a so the two shots rhyme. The shopkeeper's hand sets one small
bottle beside the phone, adjusts it slightly, and withdraws. The phone does not move. The action
is finished by second three.

### 11 · S13 Sack before the rain (10 s)
Locked-off, shallow focus. The hands work at the knot on the sack for the first three seconds.
The phone on the grass lights up and vibrates, a tiny buzz on the grass. The hands pause, rest a
beat, then tie the knot shut again and pat the sack once. Behind, dark clouds move slowly left
and the wind builds in the rice, bending it a little further by the end. No rain yet.

### 12 · S15a Potato field (5 s)
Locked-off, shallow focus. The hand holds the phone steady, listening, with a slight natural
sway; the sari's anchal stirs in the breeze. The background field stays a soft blur.

### 13 · S15b Mustard field (5 s)
Locked-off. The hand holds the phone steady; the yellow mustard flowers behind sway in gentle
wind; the shawl edge moves slightly. Same framing and hand height as 12 and 14 so the three cut
together as a set.

### 14 · S15c Pond at dusk (5 s)
Locked-off. The hand holds the phone steady; soft ripples cross the pond and the reflected dusk
light shimmers. Palm silhouettes stay still. Same framing and hand height as 12 and 13.

### 15 · S16 Tumpa at harvest (10 s)
The same camera move as clip 0, mirrored in meaning: a slow dolly-in along the ridge at walking
pace, horizon level. The girl walks away from us at a child's easy pace, her plaits swinging
lightly, the backpack bouncing a little. The heavy golden panicles sway; warm haze and a low sun
flare hold steady. The last shot of live action in the film: it should feel like the breath out.

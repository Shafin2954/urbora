# Urbora video v5: recording sheet (Jawat)

Record these lines on your phone. I will convert your recordings into the narrator and character voices with
voice-changer. Your accent, rhythm and pronunciation stay exactly as you speak them, so speak the way you
naturally talk. If a word feels unnatural in your mouth, change it and just tell me which.

## How to record (5 minutes)
- A quiet room, fan off, phone 15–20 cm from your mouth, the phone's normal voice recorder (m4a is fine).
- One file for all lines is fine. Say each line **twice**, with a **2-second pause** between takes and between
  lines. No need to say the line numbers; I match them by transcription.
- Energy: a little more lively than normal talk (ads sound flat if you read slowly). Smile slightly on the
  narrator lines.
- Attach the file(s) in your next message, together with the 4 music tracks.

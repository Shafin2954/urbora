# GP FutureMakers 2026 — what the rules say (read 2026-09-29 from gpfuturemakers.com FAQ)

Source: https://gpfuturemakers.com (111-item FAQ, expanded and read in full). Paraphrased.
On 2026-09-29 the site's countdown showed **~1 day 23 h** (label: "The forge is waiting";

## Round 1 (online submission) — what must be uploaded
- Category (we: **Digital Inclusion & Education** — its examples name "reduce language /
  literacy / accessibility barriers" and "support underserved / remote communities").
- Problem statement, target group, AI-enabled solution, **specific role of AI**, expected
  social impact, feasibility, implementation approach.
- **2-minute video pitch — mandatory.** MP4, ≤ 2 min, ≤ 300 MB, Bangla or English, one member
  may present; slides / screen recordings / mock-ups allowed; a working app is NOT required
  (but a real demo helps judges understand).
- Team documents: every member's **student ID** (or admission receipt / ID expired on or
  after 30 Jun 2026) and an **updated CV**. Team of 1–3, finalised before submission.
- Existing ideas allowed if owned; **prior development, awards, funding, institutional or
  third-party contributions must be disclosed** (→ disclose the July AgriSense hackathon build).
- Submit early; keep the submission confirmation.

## How AI must be explained ("Is adding a chatbot enough? Not necessarily.")
What the AI does, the inputs/data it uses, the output, why AI is appropriate, how it improves
the solution, limitations, risks, ethics. → Our answer: speech (Bangla STT/TTS) and language
understanding make the service usable without literacy or a smartphone; the agent plans and
explains; **deterministic, tested code** does the arithmetic, ranking and pest diagnosis
table, so the AI never invents a number or a dose.

## Judging criteria (7)
1 Originality & creativity · 2 Problem relevance · 3 AI & technology integration ·
4 Impact potential · 5 Feasibility & clarity · 6 Video-pitch quality · 7 Responsible &
ethical AI use. Complexity does not score by itself.

## Later stages
Concept Pitch (≤12 slides, 8 min + 7 min Q&A; deck must cover team, problem, target group,
evidence, solution, role of AI, user journey, impact, feasibility, **financial
considerations**, go-to-market, scalability/roadmap) → Ignition Chamber (mentoring) →
Grand Finale. Prizes: BDT 300k / 200k / 100k.

## Terms worth knowing
- **All IP in submitted ideas is Grameenphone's exclusively until the programme ends; GP
  keeps a right of first refusal afterwards.** The team should read the T&C before submitting.
- Personal data: use anonymised / synthetic / authorised data (→ our demo rows are labelled,
  phone numbers hashed).
- Disqualification: plagiarism, undisclosed third-party content, non-AI submission, missed deadline.
